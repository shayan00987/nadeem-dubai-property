GOOD QUESTIONS FIRST

Frequently asked questions.

Can foreign nationals buy property in Dubai?+

Foreign ownership depends on the location and property rules. Confirm eligibility and legal details for the specific property with qualified local professionals.

Do you work with off-plan and ready properties?+

The profile identifies off-plan and secondary-market property as Nadeem's specialist areas. Ask directly about current options and availability.

Is AED 3,500 per month enough to buy a property?+

The Instagram profile mentions options from AED 3,500/month. This is not a guarantee of eligibility or a complete cost estimate; payment plans, deposits, fees and terms vary by property.

Can I pay online for a consultation?+

Contact Nadeem to confirm whether a paid consultation is available and which payment methods are currently supported. This website does not collect or process payments.
